# Privacy Policy — Route Logger

**Effective date:** August 2026

Route Logger ("the App") is a delivery route and payout tracker. This policy explains what data the App uses and how it is handled.

## The short version

**Your data never leaves your phone.** Route Logger stores everything locally on your device. We do not operate a server, we do not collect, sell, or share your data, and the App does not require an account.

## Data the App stores

The App keeps the following information **only on your device**, in local storage:

- **Stores** you add (store number, name, address)
- **Trips and stops** you log (arrival/departure times, miles, stop counts, pro numbers, notes)
- **Your payout rates** (per trailer, per stop, per mile)
- **App settings** (e.g., whether you've completed setup)

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

## Location

Route Logger uses your device's location for two purposes:

1. **Auto-timer (geofencing):** if you enable the Auto-Timer, the App uses a geofence around your warehouse to automatically start your timer when you leave and stop it when you return. This may require background location access.
2. **Optional:** you may choose to record a store's location.

Location data is used only to operate these features on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere.

**You control this.** Background location is optional. If you don't grant it, the Auto-Timer simply won't run in the background — you can still start and stop the timer manually.

## Notifications

The App may show notifications to confirm the Auto-Timer started or stopped. These are generated on your device.

## What the App does NOT do

- Does not send your data to any server
- Does not collect analytics, ads, or usage tracking
- Does not require an account or login
- Does not share data with third parties
- Does not sell data

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves. If we ever add a feature that sends data off your device, we will update this policy and tell you before it ships.
